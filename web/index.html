04

Predict 30-day nonclosure

Compare statistical prediction models on a later time period—not the training records.

Creation-time predictors only: service, borough, creation weekday and hour. Closure and current status are never used as predictors. The model is not a verified assessment of actual service resolution.
Requires at least 40 historical training cases, 20 later test cases, both outcome classes, and a full follow-up gap between training and testing.

Higher ROC-AUC is better; lower Brier score and log loss are better. Accuracy is reported at a fixed threshold of 50%. Baseline comparison is essential. Results are exploratory, not certified predictive performance.
